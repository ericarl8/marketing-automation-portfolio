# 📌 Project Overview

The Customer Order Notification Automation streamlines the process of receiving and managing new customer orders.

Instead of manually checking for new orders and notifying staff, customers submit their orders through Google Forms. The automation records the order in Google Sheets, immediately notifies the business team, and sends an order confirmation email to the customer.

This workflow demonstrates how automation can improve communication, reduce manual work, and help businesses respond to customer orders more efficiently.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Small businesses often process customer orders manually.

Common challenges include:

- Manually recording new orders
- Delayed notifications to staff
- Slow customer confirmation
- Risk of missed or overlooked orders
- Time-consuming administrative work

These manual processes can affect customer satisfaction and slow down daily operations.

---

# 💡 Solution

I designed an automated order notification workflow using Make.com to simplify the entire process.

When a customer submits an order:

- The order information is captured automatically.
- The order is saved to Google Sheets.
- The business team receives an email notification.
- The customer receives an order confirmation email.

The workflow operates automatically, ensuring every order is recorded and acknowledged without manual intervention.

---

# 🔄 Workflow

```text
Google Forms
      │
      ▼
Google Sheets
      │
      ▼
Gmail (Business Notification)
      │
      ▼
Gmail (Customer Confirmation)
```

# 📌 Project Overview

The AI Social Media Content Generator automates the creation of social media content from a single content request.

Instead of manually brainstorming ideas, writing captions, and formatting content for publication, users submit a content request through Google Sheets. The workflow automatically generates engaging social media copy using Google Gemini AI and saves the completed content to Google Docs.

This automation demonstrates how AI and workflow automation can simplify content creation while helping marketing teams produce consistent, high-quality content more efficiently.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Creating social media content requires creativity, planning, and time.

Marketing teams often spend hours:

- Brainstorming content ideas
- Writing captions
- Creating promotional copy
- Formatting documents
- Organizing completed content

These repetitive tasks reduce productivity and limit the amount of content that can be created.

---

# 💡 Solution

I designed an AI-powered automation using Make.com that simplifies social media content creation.

After a content request is submitted, the automation:

- Captures the content request
- Sends the request to Google Gemini AI
- Generates engaging social media content
- Creates a Google Document containing the generated copy

The entire workflow is completed automatically, allowing marketing teams to focus more on strategy and audience engagement.

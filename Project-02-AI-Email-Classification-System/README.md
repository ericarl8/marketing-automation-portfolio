# 📌 Project Overview

The AI Email Classification System automates the process of organizing incoming emails by using Google Gemini AI to identify their purpose and categorize them automatically.

Instead of manually reviewing every email, the workflow analyzes the email content, determines its category, and stores the information in Google Sheets for easier tracking and organization.

This automation demonstrates how AI can improve email management, reduce repetitive administrative work, and help businesses respond more efficiently.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses receive many emails every day from customers, clients, and partners.

Manually reviewing each email can lead to:

- Slow response times
- Disorganized inboxes
- Missed important messages
- Increased administrative workload
- Difficulty tracking customer inquiries

As email volume increases, managing communications manually becomes less efficient.

---

# 💡 Solution

I designed an AI-powered email classification workflow using Make.com that automatically processes incoming Gmail messages.

When a new email is received, the automation:

- Monitors the Gmail inbox
- Sends the email content to Google Gemini AI
- Identifies the email category
- Records the results in Google Sheets

The workflow creates an organized email database that helps businesses manage communications more efficiently.

# 📌 Project Overview

The AI Marketing Report Generator automates the creation of weekly marketing reports by transforming raw campaign data into clear, AI-generated summaries.

Instead of manually reviewing spreadsheets and writing reports, marketing teams simply update their campaign data in Google Sheets. The automation processes the information, generates a professional marketing summary using Google Gemini AI, creates a Google Document, and sends the completed report to the team through Gmail.

This workflow demonstrates how AI and automation can simplify reporting, improve consistency, and reduce the time spent on repetitive documentation.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Marketing teams regularly prepare campaign reports to evaluate performance and communicate results.

Creating these reports manually often requires:

- Reviewing marketing data
- Writing summaries manually
- Formatting documents
- Sharing reports through email
- Repeating the same process every reporting period

These repetitive tasks consume valuable time that could be spent on campaign optimization and strategic planning.

---

# 💡 Solution

I designed an AI-powered reporting workflow using Make.com that automates the entire reporting process.

Once marketing data is available in Google Sheets, the automation:

- Reads the latest marketing data
- Sends the information to Google Gemini AI
- Generates a professional marketing summary
- Creates a formatted Google Document
- Emails the completed report to the marketing team

The entire reporting process is completed automatically with minimal manual effort.

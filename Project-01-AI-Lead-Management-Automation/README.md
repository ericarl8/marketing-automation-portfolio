# 📌 Project Overview

The AI Lead Management Automation streamlines the process of collecting and organizing new business leads.

Instead of manually recording lead information, assigning identifiers, formatting customer data, and sending confirmation emails, this workflow automatically processes every new lead submission from start to finish.

Using Make.com and Google Workspace, the automation creates a structured lead database, generates a unique Lead ID, formats customer information consistently, records timestamps, and sends a professional thank-you email to the potential customer.

This project demonstrates how workflow automation can simplify lead management and improve operational efficiency.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Many businesses still manage new leads manually.

This often involves:

- Copying information into spreadsheets
- Assigning lead IDs manually
- Checking for duplicate submissions
- Formatting customer information
- Recording submission dates
- Sending follow-up emails individually

These repetitive tasks consume valuable time and increase the likelihood of human error.

---

# 💡 Solution

I designed an automated lead management workflow using Make.com that processes every new lead automatically.

When a customer submits the lead form, the automation:

- Captures the submitted information
- Checks for duplicate email addresses
- Generates a unique Lead ID
- Formats customer names consistently
- Records the submission timestamp
- Stores the lead in Google Sheets
- Sends a professional HTML thank-you email

The workflow creates a reliable and organized lead management process with minimal manual effort.

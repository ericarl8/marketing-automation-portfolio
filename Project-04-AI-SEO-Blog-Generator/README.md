# 📌 Project Overview

The AI SEO Blog Generator automates the process of creating SEO-friendly blog articles from a single keyword.

Instead of manually researching topics, creating outlines, writing content, and formatting documents, users simply submit a keyword. The automation uses Google Gemini AI to generate a complete blog article and automatically saves the finished content to Google Docs.

This workflow demonstrates how AI and automation can significantly reduce the time required to produce high-quality marketing content.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Creating SEO blog articles requires significant time and effort.

Marketing teams and business owners often spend hours:

- Researching keywords
- Creating blog outlines
- Writing long-form content
- Formatting documents
- Organizing completed articles

These repetitive tasks slow down content production and reduce overall marketing efficiency.

---

# 💡 Solution

I designed an AI-powered workflow using Make.com that automates SEO blog creation.

After a keyword is submitted, the automation:

- Captures the requested keyword
- Sends it to Google Gemini AI
- Generates an SEO-friendly blog article
- Creates a formatted Google Document containing the generated content

The workflow eliminates repetitive manual work while producing consistent blog drafts in just a few moments.

# 📌 Project Overview

The Customer Review Monitoring Automation streamlines the process of collecting customer feedback and notifying business teams whenever a new review is submitted.

Instead of manually checking customer feedback forms or spreadsheets, this automation captures reviews submitted through Google Forms, stores them in Google Sheets, and instantly sends a Slack notification to the team.

This workflow demonstrates how automation can improve customer feedback management and help businesses respond more quickly to customer experiences.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Many businesses rely on customer feedback to improve their products and services.

However, manually monitoring reviews can lead to:

- Delayed responses to customer feedback
- Missed customer concerns
- Disorganized review records
- Time-consuming manual monitoring
- Slow communication within the team

Without timely notifications, valuable customer insights may not be acted on quickly.

---

# 💡 Solution

I designed an automated review monitoring workflow using Make.com that instantly processes new customer reviews.

When a customer submits a review:

- The review is captured through Google Forms.
- The information is stored in Google Sheets.
- A Slack notification is sent to the team with the review details.

This automation ensures every customer review is organized and immediately visible to the business team.

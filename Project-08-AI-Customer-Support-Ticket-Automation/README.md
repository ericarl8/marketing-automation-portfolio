# 📌 Project Overview

The AI Customer Support Ticket Automation streamlines the process of managing customer support emails by automatically analyzing incoming messages using Google Gemini AI.

Instead of manually reviewing every email, the automation identifies the customer's issue, determines its priority, categorizes the request, and stores the information in Google Sheets for easy tracking and management.

This workflow helps businesses respond faster while maintaining an organized customer support system.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses often receive large volumes of customer emails every day.

Manually reviewing each email to determine its urgency and category can be time-consuming and may result in delayed responses or overlooked requests.

Without an organized process, support teams may struggle to prioritize critical issues and maintain accurate records.

---

# 💡 Solution

I designed an AI-powered customer support automation using Make.com that automatically processes every new customer email.

The workflow:

- Detects new emails in Gmail
- Sends the email content to Google Gemini AI
- Identifies the support reason
- Determines the ticket priority
- Stores the processed ticket in Google Sheets

The result is an organized support ticket database that enables businesses to respond more efficiently.
